Mentorship for Exceptional Students

For students whose intellectual curiosity goes beyond the classroom, I offer ongoing one-to-one mentorship in the tradition of the private tutor: a sustained programme of reading, conversation, and guided inquiry across literature, history, philosophy, and theology.

The aim is not to prepare for any particular exam, but to cultivate the habits of serious thought – the kind of thing that was once considered essential for any able young mind, and that schools, however good, are rarely structured to provide.

Programmes are designed around the individual student, and typically run alongside their regular schooling.
